Everything in one reader

RSS, Atom & scraping

Paste a feed address, or just the site's, and Readfine goes looking for the feed itself. YouTube channels included. A site with no feed at all can be scraped with a CSS selector.

Save anything you find

Paste a link to keep an article that is in none of your feeds, or share one to Readfine from any other app on Android. It lands in Saved, cleaned up like everything else, and stays there until you remove it.

Filters

Match on title, content, author or URL, with regex and AND/OR where plain "contains" runs out, then label, star, mark read or archive. Any filter can be kept to certain feeds or folders.

Relevance scoring

Say what you are interested in, or let the AI draft it from what you actually read, and every new article gets a score. A filter can then mark the low ones read before you ever see them.

Clean reading view

The full article text is pulled into the reader, without the cookie banners and sidebars of the original page. Feeds that carry only a headline offer the source instead of an empty pane.

Videos play in the reader

A YouTube or Vimeo link plays where it sits, with the whole description under it. No player loads until you press play, and the thumbnail comes from your own server, so the video site hears about you only when you decide to watch.

AI on your own keys

A summary of any article, the background it assumes you already know, and a chat about it. Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini on your own key, stored encrypted and used for nobody else.

Catch me up & briefings

Ask what happened in your feeds while you were away, over whatever period and feeds you pick. The same digest can arrive in your inbox on a schedule instead.

Reads like an app

Put it on a phone or a desktop and it opens in its own window. Two or three panels on a big screen, a proper small-screen layout on a phone, dark mode, and search on the / key.

How it works

  1. STEP 1

    Subscribe

    Paste a feed address, or the site's own and let Readfine find it.

  2. STEP 2

    Auto-fetch

    Readfine pulls new articles on a schedule, in the background.

  3. STEP 3

    Filter & score

    Your filters and AI scoring sort the relevant articles from the rest.

  4. STEP 4

    Read & ask

    Read the clean view, get a summary, or chat about the article.

Open source, and yours to run

Self-host it

Readfine is free and AGPL licensed, so you can read every line of it. Run it on your own server with Docker and your feeds, articles and keys never leave the machine. Or sign up here and let us host it for you.

Run the AI locally too

The AI part doesn't have to be somebody's cloud either. Point Readfine at anything that speaks the OpenAI API: Ollama or llama.cpp on your own machine, vLLM, a LiteLLM proxy, a gateway like OpenRouter. A local server needs no key at all and nothing leaves your network.
