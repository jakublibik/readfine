{# ── Basic relevance ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Basic relevance

Runs on everything that arrives, from the title and the first 300 characters, with no API key and no cost. It is a weaker signal than AI scoring: it matches words, so it cannot tell that an article about “LLM jailbreaks” is about AI safety unless your profile says one of those words. Measured against what people actually read, it ranks clearly better than nothing and clearly worse than the AI score. Does nothing while the interest profile is empty.
