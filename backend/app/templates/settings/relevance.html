{# ── Basic relevance ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Basic relevance

Runs on everything that arrives, from the title and the first 300 characters, with no API key and no cost. It is a weaker signal than AI scoring: it matches words, so it cannot tell that a report from the Tour de France is about cycling unless one of your terms appears in it. Measured against what people actually read, it ranks clearly better than nothing and clearly worse than the AI score.
