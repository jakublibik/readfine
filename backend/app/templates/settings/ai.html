{# ── Limits ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Limits

characters

Maximum characters of normalized article text (HTML stripped, whitespace collapsed) sent to AI for summaries, context, and chat. Higher values improve quality for long articles at the cost of more tokens. Default: 20 000.
