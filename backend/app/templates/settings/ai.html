{# ── Summaries & context ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Summaries & context

Auto-generate AI summaries for starred articles using the main model. Per-feed override available in feed settings.

{# Indented and dimmed with its checkbox, like the scoring block above: the threshold governs the automatic runs and nothing else. #}
characters

Articles shorter than this are not summarized automatically, there is too little left to condense. Counts normalized text (HTML stripped, whitespace collapsed) including the title. The Summarize and Context buttons ignore it, so a short article you want a summary of is always one click away. Applies from now on, it does not fill in summaries for articles you starred earlier. Default: {{ ai_min_chars_default | thousands }}.

Users can add a per-article focus when generating context.

{# ── Chat ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Chat

Enables AI chat - accessible from the sidebar, mobile toolbar, and article menu. Supports general conversation and article-specific context ("Include article" sends up to the configured content limit). Conversation history (up to 5 turns) is sent with each message.

{# ── Limits ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Limits

characters

Maximum characters of normalized article text (HTML stripped, whitespace collapsed) sent to AI for summaries, context, and chat. Higher values improve quality for long articles at the cost of more tokens. Default: {{ ai_content_limit_default | thousands }}.
