{#- The invisible ticker that keeps a saved article's list row up to date. Such a row is inserted under a placeholder title (the pasted address) and only learns its real one when extraction finishes, which happens after the row was rendered. This polls until then; the endpoint answers with itself while the extraction runs and with the rebuilt row once it is done, retargeted at the row, so the polling stops with the swap. A macro because the poll endpoint has to emit the very same element to keep the loop going, and an element that hands itself back must be defined once or the two copies will disagree about the interval, the target or the click guard. Scoped by its callers to articles with no feed: a feed article already has its title from the feed, so its row would poll for a change that never comes. Every value is url-encoded: the poll endpoint echoes back whatever it was given, so one of them arriving with an & in it would otherwise split into a parameter of its own on the next tick. story_parent is the row a member was unfolded from, empty for an ordinary row. It travels with the poll because the rebuilt row has to come back indented and still marked as part of its group. hx-target="this" is load-bearing, not decoration. hx-target is inherited, and this element lives inside the article row, which targets #article-detail — so without it the first tick swapped the poller's own answer, outerHTML, over the whole detail panel and took it out of the page. Every later row click then had no target left to open into, and only a reload brought the panel back. -#} {%- macro row_poll(article_id, density, label_display, story_parent="") -%}
{%- endmacro -%}
